Vibe Shell 隐私政策 / Privacy Policy

更新日期 / Last updated: 2026-10-19

English Version ↓

1. 默认不收集任何数据

Vibe Shell 默认不收集、上传或分享任何个人信息或使用数据。App 内没有统计 SDK、没有广告、没有账号系统。崩溃记录与连接诊断记录只保存在您的设备上。

2. 可选的诊断日志上传（默认关闭）

只有当您在「设置 → 诊断 → 上传诊断日志」中手动开启后，App 才会把诊断日志发送到开发者的日志服务器，仅用于排查连接失败、卡顿、断线等问题。上传内容包括：

  • App 自行随机生成的设备标识（不是广告标识符，也无法关联到您的身份）、设备型号、系统版本与 App 版本；
  • 连接诊断日志，其中可能包含您连接的服务器地址、端口、用户名与 Tailscale 节点名；
  • 崩溃记录与系统提供的性能统计。

上传内容从不包含密码、私钥、密钥口令、终端输入输出内容；网址中的 token 等参数在写入日志前即被抹除。这些数据不会与任何第三方共享，也不会用于广告或追踪。您可以随时在同一处关闭上传。

3. 您的连接信息只存在您的设备与您的 iCloud 里

服务器地址、端口、用户名等连接信息保存在设备本地；密码、私钥与密钥口令存放于系统钥匙串（Keychain）。

开启「设置 → 数据与安全 → iCloud 同步」后，节点信息会通过您自己的 iCloud 账户在 iPhone、iPad、Mac 之间保持一致，凭据存放在 iCloud 钥匙串（由 Apple 端到端加密，我们无法读取）。这些数据不经过我们的服务器。随时可在同一处关闭同步。

4. 网络连接只去往您指定的服务器

App 发起的 SSH、SFTP、VNC、端口转发等连接都指向您自己填写的服务器。如果您选择登录 Tailscale，连接协商经由 Tailscale 官方协调服务器进行，该过程遵循 Tailscale 隐私政策；我们不从中获取任何数据。

5. 命令助手与错误诊断完全离线

命令建议、报错翻译等智能功能全部在本机通过规则匹配完成，您的终端内容不会被发送到任何服务器。

6. 权限说明

App 不申请定位、相册、相机、麦克风、通讯录等系统权限。局域网访问仅用于连接您自己的服务器。

7. 政策更新与联系我们

如本政策有修改，更新后的内容将在此页面公布。如有疑问，请联系：liling.developer@gmail.com

1. Nothing is collected by default

By default, Vibe Shell does not collect, upload, or share any personal information or usage data. There are no analytics SDKs, no ads, and no account system. Crash records and connection diagnostics stay on your device.

2. Optional diagnostics upload (off by default)

Only if you turn on "Settings → Diagnostics → Upload Diagnostics Logs" does the app send diagnostics logs to the developer's log server, used solely to troubleshoot connection failures, lag, and disconnects. An upload contains:

  • a random identifier generated by the app (not the advertising identifier, and not linked to your identity), device model, OS version and app version;
  • connection diagnostics, which may include the addresses, ports, usernames and Tailscale node names of the servers you connect to;
  • crash records and performance statistics provided by the system.

Uploads never include passwords, private keys, key passphrases, or terminal input/output; tokens and other parameters in web addresses are stripped before anything is logged. This data is never shared with third parties or used for advertising or tracking. You can turn uploading off at any time in the same place.

3. Your connection details stay on your device and in your iCloud

Connection details (hosts, ports, usernames) are stored locally on your device; passwords, private keys and key passphrases live in the system Keychain.

With "Settings → Data & Security → iCloud Sync" enabled, nodes stay consistent across your iPhone, iPad and Mac through your own iCloud account, and credentials are stored in iCloud Keychain (end-to-end encrypted by Apple — we cannot read them). None of it passes through our servers. You can turn sync off at any time in the same place.

4. Network connections go only where you point them

SSH, SFTP, VNC and port-forwarding connections target the servers you configure. If you choose to sign in to Tailscale, connection coordination goes through Tailscale's official coordination servers under Tailscale's privacy policy; we receive nothing from it.

5. Command Assistant and error explanations run fully offline

Command suggestions and error explanations are produced on-device by local rule matching. Your terminal content is never sent anywhere.

6. Permissions

The app requests no system permissions such as location, photos, camera, microphone, or contacts. Local network access is used solely to reach your own servers.

7. Changes and contact

Any changes to this policy will be posted on this page. Questions? Contact liling.developer@gmail.com
