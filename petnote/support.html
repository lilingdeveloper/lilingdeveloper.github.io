宠备忘 技术支持与常见问题

适用于 iOS 与 iPadOS 版本宠备忘（PetNote）

1. iCloud 家人共享（主人 / 辅助照护人）如何使用？

宠备忘通过 Apple iCloud（CKShare）进行家庭协同，双方设备均需登录各自的 Apple 账户并开启 iCloud 云盘：

主人（Owner）：打开右上角「设置」，在共享区域生成 iCloud 邀请链接并发送给家人。主人负责管理宠物档案、常用口粮克数预设以及日常药箱预设。

辅助照护人（Helper）：在已安装宠备忘的 iPhone 或 iPad 上打开邀请链接即可免费加入（无需再次购买解锁）。辅助照护人可以新增和维护每日排便排尿、喂食喂药、体重与照护备注，同时不会误改主人设定好的口粮配方或药箱剂量。

2. 更换手机或重装应用后，如何恢复一次性买断权益？

请确认新设备在系统「设置 → App Store」中登录的是当初购买时使用的同一个 Apple 账户。打开宠备忘右上角「设置」，点击买断说明卡片中的「恢复购买」，系统将通过 Apple StoreKit 2 自动校验并恢复多宠与共享发起权限，不会重复扣费。

3. 如何导入以前的 Excel、Numbers、CSV 表格或历史病历？

打开右上角「设置」中的历史数据导入入口，从系统文件选择器或相册中选取 Excel（.xlsx）、Numbers、CSV、PDF 文档或以往记录截图。应用会在手机本地自动解析日期、饮食、排泄、体重或化验单文字，供您核对后归入当前宠物记录本。

4. 数据保存在哪里，如何备份？

所有记录与病例附件首先保存在设备本地的 SQLite 数据库中，离线状态下也可正常记录与翻阅。只要您的设备开启了 Apple iCloud 且存储空间充足，应用会自动将数据同步备份至您私有的 iCloud 空间；在登录同一 Apple 账户的新设备上安装宠备忘后，历史记录会自动同步回来。

5. 共享记录没有及时出现在家人手机上怎么办？

请依次检查双方设备：是否处于正常联网状态、系统「设置 → Apple 账户 → iCloud」中是否为宠备忘开启了同步开关、个人 iCloud 剩余空间是否已满，以及是否开启了「低电量模式」或「低数据模式」（系统在省电模式下可能会延后后台 CloudKit 同步）。重新打开应用稍候片刻即可完成同步。

PetNote Technical Support & FAQ

For PetNote on iOS & iPadOS

1. How does Apple iCloud Owner & Helper sharing work?

PetNote coordinates household care through Apple CloudKit (CKShare). Both participants should be signed into their own Apple Accounts on iOS or iPadOS with iCloud Drive enabled:

Owner: Open the top-right Settings sheet, start family sharing to generate an iCloud invitation link, and send it to your family member or sitter. The Owner manages pet profiles, food portion gram presets, and medication cabinet presets.

Family Helper: Tap the invitation link on a device with PetNote installed to join at no cost (no separate purchase needed). Helpers can view history and log or edit daily stool, urine, meals, medications, weight, and care notes without risking accidental changes to food or medication presets.

2. How do I restore my one-time lifetime purchase on a new device?

Make sure your device is signed into the same Apple Account used for the original purchase. Open PetNote, tap the top-right Settings button, and tap "Restore Purchases". Apple StoreKit 2 verifies your lifetime unlock directly with no additional charge.

3. How do I import Excel, Numbers, CSV spreadsheets, or past documents?

Open Settings inside PetNote and choose Import Records, then select your Excel (.xlsx), Numbers, CSV, PDF file, or past log screenshots from the system picker or photo library. Parsing and Apple Vision OCR run locally on your device so you can review and save entries into the selected pet notebook.

4. How is my data backed up?

All entries and veterinary attachments are saved locally in SQLite on your device for reliable offline access. When iCloud is turned on with available storage, PetNote automatically backs up and syncs your notebook to your private Apple iCloud container. Signing into the same Apple Account on a new iPhone or iPad restores your synced data automatically.

5. Why is iCloud sync taking a moment between shared devices?

Please check on both devices that internet connectivity is active, iCloud sync is enabled for PetNote under Settings → Apple Account → iCloud, neither iCloud account is out of storage, and Low Power Mode or Low Data Mode is not pausing background CloudKit transfers. Reopening PetNote with an active connection resumes sync shortly.
